Requires Inventory Plus 1.9.0 or newer, Keybindery 1.1.0 or newer, and MenuKit and MenuKit: Containers 6.1.0 or newer, below 7.0.0.

Pockets, Equipment Slots and Mend Anywhere each have an on and off switch in Inventory Plus's column beside your inventory. The three switches start hidden. To show one, tick Show the Pockets button, Show the Equipment Slots button or Show the Mend Anywhere button on that feature's tab. Left-click a switch to turn its feature on or off, or Ctrl-click it (Cmd on a Mac) to open its tab.

You can now turn off the elytra slot and the totem slot separately, on the Equipment Slots tab. Turning one off hides that slot and stops what it does, and the other keeps working as before.

The pocket row above a hotbar slot, and its plus and minus buttons, now draw on top of Inventory Plus's buttons, so your clicks there reach the pockets.
