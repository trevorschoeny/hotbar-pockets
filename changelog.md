## 2.1.1

#### Fixed

- Pockets now show on every container screen, not only your inventory, with the plus and minus buttons working there too.
- Shrinking a pocket in creative no longer deletes the item inside it.

#### Changed

- Pocket counts are now kept on the server instead of your computer; a world's existing count carries over the first time you join it after updating.
- Shift-clicking into a pocket now follows Inventory Plus's Reach setting, off by default, instead of always being blocked.
- Requires SlotKit 2.3.0 or newer.

### 2.1.0

#### Changed

- Requires MenuKit 7.4.0 or newer and SlotKit 2.1.0 or newer.
- The cycle keys now work on the creative inventory's Survival Inventory tab. Hover a hotbar slot or its pockets there and press a key, as in your survival inventory.
- Works with Inventory Plus 3.3.0 or newer. Older versions can't be installed alongside it.
- Pockets are now protected in Reach by default: closed to everything but your own clicks, shift-click out, and the pocket turn itself.
