## 2.0.0

Inventory Max is now Hotbar Pockets, and it does one thing: the pockets behind your hotbar.

The elytra slot, the totem slot, Mend Anywhere and shared container locks are gone from this mod. The elytra slot, the totem slot and Mend Anywhere come back later as their own mods, Simple Elytra Slot, Simple Totem Slot and Mend Anywhere. Inventory Plus's container locks still work, kept on your computer.

Anything left in Inventory Max's pockets, elytra slot or totem slot comes back the first time you join a world after updating. It goes into your inventory, and whatever doesn't fit drops at your feet.

Hotbar Pockets saves under its own name, so each world starts with no pockets, and your keys and settings start at their defaults.

Inventory Plus is optional now. With Inventory Plus 3.0.0 or newer, Restock takes from your pockets, Reach lists them as Pockets, and the settings are on a Pockets tab in Inventory Plus's settings menu. Without it the defaults apply. Keybindery is no longer needed.

Shift-clicks no longer send items into a pocket, and double-click collecting and drag filling skip pockets. Shift-clicking a pocket still empties it into your inventory.

Items in pockets no longer mend from XP.

Requires MenuKit 7.1.0 or newer, below 8.0.0, and SlotKit 1.1.0 or newer, below 2.0.0.
