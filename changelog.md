Requires Inventory Plus 1.9.0 or newer, Keybindery 1.1.0 or newer, and MenuKit and MenuKit: Containers 6.1.0 or newer, below 7.0.0.

Pockets, Equipment Slots and Mend Anywhere each get an on/off switch in Inventory Plus's column beside your inventory. Left-click a switch to turn its feature on or off, or Ctrl-click it (Cmd-click on a Mac) to open that feature's tab. Each tab has a checkbox to show or hide its switch.

You can now turn off the elytra slot and the totem slot separately, on the Equipment Slots tab. Turning one off hides that slot and stops what it does. The other slot keeps working as before.

The pocket row above a hotbar slot, and its plus and minus buttons, now draw on top of Inventory Plus's buttons, so your clicks there reach the pockets.
